# Cookbook Ring Modulator

Multiplying by a sine moves every partial up and down by the carrier's frequency: at a few hertz it is a tremolo, in the audio range it is the metallic sound of a ring modulator.

**Ask for:** "a ring modulator whose carrier follows the notes I play"

Start from it: `est new ring-mod <folder> --id <your id> --name "<your name>"`, then `est validate` and `est test` (see the SDK's `docs/cookbook.md`).
